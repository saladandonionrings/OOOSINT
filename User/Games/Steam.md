>[!INFO] About investigating Steam accounts
>A Steam account can expose an extensive web of interconnected data. By analyzing their public profile, you can pivot to uncover connected social media accounts, alternative aliases (via alias history), regional locations, and highly specific active hours. Furthermore, mapping a target’s friend list and group memberships often reveals real-world social circles and close associates, making Steam a powerful starting point for behavioral and network analysis.
